All 8 Roblox Revenue Streams — Controls, Mechanics & Examples

Baseline presets conservatively include only Streams #1–#2. Enable or customize Streams #3–#8 below to model full live-ops monetization.

Stream #1 · Baseline Core

In-Game IAP (GamePasses & DevProducts)

$0

Direct Robux purchases for Season Passes, Sidegrade Kits, Skin Cases, Starter Packs, and Key Bundles. Roblox deducts a 30% platform fee; the studio keeps 70% Earned Robux and converts at DevEx ($0.0038/R$, or $0.0054/R$ for verified U.S. 18+). Controlled via M3/M24 ARPDAU and Direct-Only Shop in Section 2.

Examples: Rivals (Skin Cases, Key Bundles) & BedWars (Kits) Roblox IAP Docs ↗
Stream #2 · Baseline Core

Creator Rewards — Daily Engagement

$0

Replaced legacy Engagement-Based Payouts. Pays a flat 5 Earned R$ ($0.019) per qualifying "Active Spender" ($9.99+ spent on Roblox in past 60 days) who plays 10+ minutes when ENGAGE is among their first 3 qualifying games that day. Controlled via Active Spender % in Section 2.

Fit: 24–26 min FPS sessions naturally clear 10m gate Creator Rewards Docs ↗
Stream #3 · Unmodeled Upside (M4+)

Experience Subscriptions ("ENGAGE Elite")

$0

Monthly auto-renewing memberships (local fiat USD or Robux). You earn 70% in Month 1 and 100% of Robux value on Month 2+ renewals (Roblox drops the 30% cut!). Ideal for a $3.99/mo VIP Club (monthly Shards stipend, Clan Tag, +10% Pass XP).

Mechanism: 70% M1 → ~90% blended renewal payout Subscriptions Docs ↗
Stream #4 · Monetizes 90%+ Non-Payers (M4+)

Rewarded Video Ads (6–30s Opt-In)

$0

Players choose to watch a 6–30s full-screen video after a match in exchange for a non-P2W Developer Product (e.g., 2× Daily Quest Shards or 1-Match Locked Kit Trial — never direct Robux). Monetizes free-to-play users who spend $0 IAP, paid by EPM.

In-Experience Avatar UGC Closet

$0

Players inspect teammates or browse a "Dark Matter Closet" in the lobby and buy Avatar Marketplace gear in-game. Roblox pays 40% commission on 3rd-party items, and 70% (40% + 30% creator) when selling ENGAGE's own Dark Matter UGC helmets/backpacks.

Native Private Servers

$0

Built-in monthly Robux server rentals (70% net share) for clan wars, 1v1 ranked scrims, friend groups, and YouTuber tournament filming without paying external server hosting bills. Includes engagement payouts when Premium friends join.

Stream #7 · CAC Rebate Engine (M4+)

Creator Rewards — Audience Expansion

$0

Pays a 35% platform rev-share over 60 days on Robux spent anywhere on Roblox by brand-new or 60-day reactivated users brought via your creator share links—effectively rebating a portion of your TikTok/YouTube influencer spend.
