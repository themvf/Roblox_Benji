Two directions for the lobby

Five screens, each drawn two ways, at desktop and phone size. These are concept mockups made in a browser. They are not screenshots of the game, the character and item art are grey placeholders, and every price is an example.

Direction A · Walk-in hub

You spawn in the lobby and can walk. The menu is a thin layer over the world. This is what the lobby does today and what the new shell code builds.

  • Nav sits clear of the thumbstick, jump button and hotbar.
  • Screens open as panels over the world.
  • Look: dark slate, gold accent, the current game font.

Direction B · Character stage

You arrive at a menu with your character in the middle. Walking the lobby is a button, not the default.

  • A large Play button in the corner and a fixed rail of destinations.
  • Screens are full pages beside the rail.
  • Look: deep violet, cyan accent, a condensed italic headline font.

What to decide

Two separate choices. You can mix them.

  • Structure: walk-in hub or character stage.
  • Look: slate and gold, or violet and cyan.

Dashed outlines mark what Roblox draws itself: the thumbstick, jump and fire buttons, and the hotbar.
