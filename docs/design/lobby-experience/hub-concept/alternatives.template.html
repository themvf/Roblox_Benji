Four other ways to build the hub

Each keeps exactly what the fifth version holds: five arrival spawns, the same five queue pads at their gameplay sizes, an ARMORY, a KITS hall, a SHOP, the leaderboard, and the lobby's own canister, stone columns and purple columns. Floor area and wall height are about the same. What changes is how the pieces are arranged around the player, and what you see in the first five seconds.

Blockout renders from Blender, built from the same parts as the concept page, with no roofs. They show layout, scale and sightlines, not final art or Roblox lighting. Walk times are straight-line distance at Roblox's default walk speed. None of these is built; the fifth version is what is in the lobby file now.

Recommendation

Overlook. It is the only layout that answers the question a new player has on arrival, "what can I play?", with one look: every pad and the gate are laid out below you. It keeps the open sky of the fifth version, needs no new systems, and the lobby markers already accept a raised arrival.

Worth combining: the fifth version's plaza with an Overlook arrival. The spawns go on a raised terrace at the back of the plaza, so the first view looks down the avenue over the pads to the gate. That keeps the layout that is already built and changes only the arrival.

Orbit is the quickest to play; Boulevard is the simplest to extend; Islands is the most memorable but needs fall handling and the most work.
