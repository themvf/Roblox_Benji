# Bouncer: one whole-body picture

The nine-piece Bouncer was rejected as "a lot of random pieces put together" (2026-11-19).
This time the whole character is made as one model from one picture, so every part shares one
texture. That model is then cut at the R15 joints (route 1) and also run through Studio's
Avatar Setup (route 2).

Make it in Midjourney with `concept.jpg` (concept C) attached as the image reference.

```
a single chunky blocky Roblox R15 game character standing straight in a symmetrical A-pose, front view, arms held straight out and down at 45 degrees with a clear gap between each arm and the body, legs straight and slightly apart with a gap between them, clenched fists, the whole body in frame from the top of the hair to the soles of the boots, a black leather bomber jacket with huge puffy rounded shoulders, a tall popped-up collar and a glowing magenta zip line down the front, baggy black leather trousers, oversized black leather gloves, chunky heavy black leather boots with thick soles, spiky swept-up black hair, glowing magenta shutter-shade sunglasses, coiled earpiece wires, a matte black featureless face, hand-painted black leather with deep creases, scuffs and worn cream edges, visible stitching, hand-painted stylized 3D game asset like a top Roblox kit, even studio lighting, plain dark navy background, no text --ar 1:1
```

What makes a picture usable (check before sending):
- arms clearly apart from the body and legs apart from each other: the cut needs daylight there;
- front view, both sides alike, nothing held in the hands;
- head to boots in frame, nothing cropped;
- it reads as the same character as concept C.

Send two or three if they differ; I pick the one that cuts cleanest.
