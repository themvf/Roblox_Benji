## How this was made (asset-from-prompt run, 2026-11-16)

- Reference: Z-Image Turbo, `--style realistic --aspect wide`, 8 seeds; prompt in
  `trials/reference/prompt.json`. Seed 7 was the best, but every seed blurred the tail rotor
  and TRELLIS turned the blur into six or more blades.
- Tail rotor fix: `reference black-hawk --edit "..."` (FLUX.1 Kontext, instruction in
  `trials/reference/edit.json`), 3 edits. Seed 102 is the reference now in `source/`.
  Rejected: 101 (two-blade V), 103 (three blades).
- Mesh: TRELLIS.2 seeds 1 and 2 from the edited reference; seed 1 picked. Seed 2 grew the tail
  rotor back to about six blades.
- Assumptions, not confirmed by the user: role decor; height 15 studs (5.1 m real height x 3),
  which makes it about 51 x 43 studs across the rotors.
- Build: the generated grid fails the cap because the fuselage sits in the middle tiles.
  `brief.json` overrides `scene` with decimate 45,000 and tile 8x4: 18 meshes, 56,575
  triangles, largest 8,353.

## Known flaws in the export

- Rotor counts checked on the export's vertices from above: four main blades (one lies over
  the tail boom and is hidden in `review/views.png`) and a four-blade tail rotor.
- Cabin door: checked with `review/audit_closeups.png`. The two light rectangles are an open
  doorway on each side with a modelled wall behind it, not holes. No view has a back-face
  patch (`review/audit.json`, worst 0%); the magenta in the hole maps is hairline seams only.
- Surface, from `review/audit.png`: fuselage panels are slightly lumpy up close, the windows
  are painted on rather than glass, and the texture is a near-uniform dark grey with little
  marking. Fine as parked scenery at a distance; weak if players can walk right up to it.
- Not checked: how it looks under Roblox lighting. The audit sheets are brightened Blender
  renders.
