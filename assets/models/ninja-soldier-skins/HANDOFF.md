The first supplied file, `sample_2026-10-18T022027.540.glb`, is the tactical gas-mask operator (`source/TacticalOperatorSource.glb`). The second, `sample_2026-10-18T211804.405.glb`, is the sci-fi sentry (`source/SciFiSentrySource.glb`). The earlier appraisal and `review/appraisal-second.json` record the source measurements. It does not solve animation: automatic rigid R15 segmentation tears the scan apart at joints. Keep the untouched sources for reference; do not treat the decimated trials as animation-ready avatars.

Clean R15 armor adaptations are saved in `exports/TacticalOperatorArmor.glb` and `exports/SciFiSentryArmor.glb`, with editable `.blend` scenes and a common `source/armor_specs.json`. R15 runtime Parts are generated from the same spec through `src/shared/CharacterSkins/Definitions.lua` and `Visuals.lua`. The exported GLBs are armor-only review/delivery artifacts, not rigged full character models. Rifles in the concept art remain separate weapon Tools.
